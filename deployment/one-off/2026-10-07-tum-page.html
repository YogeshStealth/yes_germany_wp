Technical University of Munich (TUM), Germany: Courses, Fees, Admissions & Requirements

The Technical University of Munich (TUM) is a public research university in Germany with a strong academic focus across engineering, natural sciences, life sciences, medicine, management, and social sciences. Founded in Munich in 1868, TUM has developed into a globally connected university with campuses and study locations across Bavaria and an international presence.

For international students, TUM offers a broad selection of Bachelor's and Master's degree programmes, including programmes taught fully or predominantly in English. However, admission requirements, language requirements, application periods, and tuition fees can vary significantly from one programme to another.

For Indian students, the application process has some additional steps that should be understood before applying. Depending on the level and programme, applicants may need APS certification, a Preliminary Documentation (VPD) from Uni-Assist, and additional tests such as GRE, GATE, or GMAT.

This guide explains the Technical University of Munich courses, tuition fees, admission requirements, application process, APS and VPD requirements, scholarships, living costs, and important considerations for Indian students.

Why Consider the Technical University of Munich?

TUM is particularly relevant for students looking for a research-oriented university with strong links between technology, science, entrepreneurship, and industry.

A broad academic portfolio

TUM combines technical and scientific education with research, entrepreneurship, and interdisciplinary study. Its academic portfolio extends beyond engineering to areas including management, life sciences, medicine, architecture, and social sciences.

This breadth also supports interdisciplinary study, allowing students to explore programmes that connect technical, scientific, and other academic fields.

Strong international student community

TUM has a substantial international student population. According to its latest published figures, international students represent 45% of total enrolment, with students coming from approximately 140 countries.

For international applicants, this provides an academic environment that includes students from a wide range of national and international backgrounds.

Research and entrepreneurship

TUM places considerable emphasis on research, innovation, and entrepreneurship. Its current published figures include seven Clusters of Excellence, 280 ERC Grants, and 115 start-ups in 2025.

For prospective students, these indicators provide context about the university's research and innovation environment. They should also examine the research groups, laboratories, curriculum, and academic opportunities associated with their chosen programme.

TUM's current ranking position

Rankings can change each year, so they should always be identified by ranking organisation and edition. According to TUM's published 2026 information:

  • QS World University Rankings 2027: 25th worldwide and 1st in Germany
  • Times Higher Education World University Rankings 2026: 27th worldwide and 1st in Germany
  • Global University Employability Ranking 2026: 13th worldwide and 5th in Europe
  • Academic Ranking of World Universities 2026: 45th worldwide

These figures represent specific ranking editions and should be treated as dated results rather than permanent characteristics of TUM.

Bachelor's vs. Master's at TUM

The application process is not identical for Bachelor's and Master's applicants.

Bachelor's degree

Bachelor's applicants generally need to demonstrate that their school-leaving qualification provides access to higher education in Germany.

International school qualifications are evaluated according to German admission requirements. International applicants may also need a Preliminary Documentation from Uni-Assist.

Some Bachelor's programmes use an aptitude assessment, while other programmes may have different admission procedures.

Master's degree

Master's applicants normally need a recognised Bachelor's degree in an appropriate academic field.

TUM does not treat a Bachelor's degree as sufficient for every Master's programme automatically. The university may assess the academic content of the previous degree, including the subjects and credits completed.

Many Master's programmes use an aptitude assessment procedure, which can include an initial document-based assessment and, depending on the programme and result, an additional interview or test.

TUM Admission Requirements for International Students

One of the most important points about TUM admission is that there is no single set of requirements applicable to every degree programme.

Applicants should check the individual programme page for:

  • Academic qualification
  • Subject-specific prerequisites
  • Language requirements
  • Application period
  • Aptitude assessment
  • Required tests
  • Documents
  • Tuition fees
  • Start semester

Academic qualification

For Bachelor's applicants, TUM generally assesses the higher education entrance qualification.

For Master's applicants, the relevant Bachelor's degree and its academic content are important.

A programme may require a particular academic background rather than simply accepting any Bachelor's degree.

Language requirements

TUM programmes can be taught in German, English, or a combination of both.

Applicants must provide the language proof specified by their individual programme.

This is why students should not assume that an English-taught programme at TUM automatically has identical language requirements to another English-taught programme.

Aptitude assessment

TUM uses different admission procedures depending on the programme. These can include:

  • Unrestricted admission
  • Aptitude assessment
  • Aptitude recommendation
  • Numerus clausus or restricted admission
  • Programme-specific Master's aptitude assessment

For Bachelor's programmes with aptitude assessment, academic grades, and subject-specific performance may be evaluated before an applicant is invited to an interview or written test, depending on the programme. For Master's programmes, TUM may evaluate Bachelor's grades and application documents in an initial stage and may then invite eligible applicants to an interview or other assessment.

How to Apply to TUM from India

The application process for studying at TUM can be understood as a sequence of steps. The exact requirements and admission procedure vary by degree programme, so applicants should always check the individual programme page before applying.

  1. Choose your degree programme

    Start with the official TUM programme page and check the details that apply to your chosen course, including:

    • Degree level
    • Subject prerequisites
    • Language requirements
    • Location
    • Duration
    • Application period
    • Admission procedure
    • Tuition fees
    • Additional tests or requirements

  2. Check your eligibility

    Review the academic and language requirements for the programme and confirm that your previous qualification meets the stated criteria.

    For Indian applicants, also check whether any additional country-specific requirements apply before proceeding with the application.

  3. Complete VPD or other required preliminary procedures

    If your programme requires a VPD (preliminary documentation), complete the process through uni-assist before the relevant TUM application deadline.

    Other country-specific procedures may also apply depending on your qualification and programme. Complete these early enough to obtain any required documentation for the TUM application or enrolment process.

  4. Prepare your TUMonline application

    Create your application in TUMonline and upload the documents required for your chosen degree programme.

    Make sure your documents are complete, authentic, and submitted in the format specified by TUM.

  5. Submit the application before the deadline

    Submit your application through TUMonline within the application period specified for your programme.

    TUM does not have one universal deadline for all degree programmes. Application periods can differ depending on the programme and semester of entry.

  6. Complete the admission procedure

    After submission, the application is reviewed according to the admission procedure for the programme. Depending on the degree, this may include:

    • Document-based evaluation
    • Aptitude assessment
    • Written tests
    • Interviews
    • Additional academic assessments

    Follow the instructions provided through TUMonline and complete any required assessment within the specified timeframe.

  7. Accept the study place and enrol

    If you receive an admission offer, follow the instructions in TUMonline to accept the study place and complete the enrolment process.

    This may include submitting additional documents and completing the required fee payments. Make sure all documents required specifically for your qualification and country of education are available before enrolment.

The key point for Indian applicants is that the application itself should be treated as a programme-specific process. APS, VPD, and any additional test or document requirements should be checked separately rather than assumed to apply identically to every TUM programme.

TUM Tuition Fees for International Students

This is an important change for international applicants.

TUM charges tuition fees to newly enrolled students from non-EU/EEA countries in Bachelor's and Master's degree programmes, subject to the university's stated exceptions.

For most programmes

  • Bachelor's tuition is usually €2,000 or €3,000 per semester
  • Master's tuition is usually €4,000 or €6,000 per semester

The exact amount is programme-specific. The tuition fee is in addition to the semester fee.

Example of programme-specific fees

  • The Bachelor's in Management and Technology at Munich currently lists tuition of €2,000 per semester for international students from third countries.
  • The Master's in Informatics currently lists €6,000 per semester.
  • The Master's in Data Engineering and Analytics also currently lists €6,000 per semester.

These examples show why students should not use a single “TUM tuition fee” figure when calculating their total cost.

Scholarships and Financial Support at TUM

TUM provides several forms of financial support, including scholarships and tuition-fee relief for eligible international students.

The TUM scholarship for international students provides one-time financial support of €500 to €1,800 per semester, with applications possible again in subsequent semesters subject to the scholarship rules.

The programme is not simply an admission scholarship. Eligibility depends on factors including academic performance and financial need, and specific semester requirements apply.

Students should also investigate:

  • TUM scholarships
  • Programme-specific funding
  • External scholarships
  • DAAD opportunities
  • Foundations and other funding organisations

Scholarship eligibility should always be checked separately from university admission eligibility.

Cost of Living in Munich

Tuition is only one part of the financial planning required for studying at TUM.

Munich is one of Germany's more expensive student locations. TUM's current general guidance estimates approximately €1,300 to €2,000 per month for a single-person household in the greater Munich area, although actual spending varies according to accommodation, lifestyle, and personal circumstances.

Typical expenses include

  • Accommodation
  • Food
  • Health insurance
  • Transportation
  • Phone and internet
  • Study materials
  • Personal expenses

Accommodation in Munich

Accommodation should be planned early. TUM does not operate its own residence halls. Many student residences are operated by the Munich Student Union and other organisations.

Demand for affordable student housing is high, and TUM advises students to start their accommodation search early. Students can consider:

  • Student residence halls
  • Shared apartments
  • Private rooms
  • Private apartments
  • Temporary accommodation during the initial period

Do not assume that admission to TUM automatically provides accommodation.

TUM is not located only in central Munich.

Munich

Weihenstephan

The Weihenstephan campus in Freising is strongly associated with life sciences and related fields.

Straubing

Heilbronn

What Makes the TUM Application Different for Indian Students?

Indian applicants to the Technical University of Munich may need to complete additional country-specific requirements alongside the regular TUM application. These can include APS verification, a VPD through Uni-Assist, and programme-specific GRE, GATE, or GMAT requirements.

The exact requirements depend on the applicant's academic background and chosen degree programme. Applicants should therefore check both TUM's country-specific requirements and the requirements of their individual programme.

APS Certificate

Applicants with Indian school or university qualifications generally need an APS certificate for admission to a German university, subject to TUM's stated exceptions.

APS, or the Academic Evaluation Center, verifies academic documents and educational qualifications from India. The applicable requirements can differ according to the applicant's academic background.

VPD Through Uni-Assist

Indian applicants to Bachelor's and Master's programmes generally need to obtain a Preliminary Documentation (VPD) through Uni-Assist, in addition to applying directly to TUM.

A VPD is not the TUM application. After receiving the VPD, applicants still need to submit their application through TUMonline and provide the required VPD documentation.

Documents for Indian Bachelor's Applicants

For the VPD process, TUM lists documents that can include:

  • School certificate
  • University entrance examination results
  • School transcript
  • APS certificate

The exact documents depend on the applicant's educational background.

Documents for Indian Master's Applicants

For Master's applicants, documents can include:

  • University diploma
  • Transcript of Records
  • APS certificate

Individual Master's programmes may require additional documents.

GRE and GATE Requirements

GRE or GATE is not required for every TUM Master's programme. Certain programmes require applicants from India to submit a current GRE or GATE result, with the applicable requirements determined by the programme.

For some programmes, graduates of an Indian Institute of Technology may be permitted to submit their IIT transcript instead of a separate GATE document where the programme's requirements allow this.

GMAT Requirements for Selected Programmes

Some TUM School of Management Master's programmes have additional requirements for applicants with Bachelor's degrees from India.

For example, the Master's in Management and Technology specifies a GMAT requirement for applicants with a Bachelor's degree from India. This requirement should not be generalised to other TUM Master's programmes.

What Indian Applicants Should Keep in Mind

The Indian application pathway can therefore involve several separate stages:

  • Academic eligibility
  • APS requirements
  • VPD through Uni-Assist, where required
  • TUMonline application
  • Programme-specific assessment
  • Admission
  • Enrolment

Not every applicant will follow exactly the same pathway. The programme's individual requirements determine which documents, tests, and assessment steps apply.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Technical University of Munich a public university?

Yes. TUM is a public research university in Germany.

Does TUM offer English-taught programmes?

Some TUM programmes are taught entirely in English, while others are taught in German or combine German and English. The language of instruction is programme-specific.

Is accommodation provided by TUM?

TUM does not operate its own student residence halls. Students need to arrange accommodation through student residences or the private housing market.

Can I apply to more than one TUM programme?

Yes. You can apply to multiple TUM programmes, but you must submit a separate application for each programme.

Does TUM offer summer-semester admission?

Yes, some TUM programmes accept applications for the summer semester. The available intake and deadline depend on the individual programme.

Do I need German if my TUM programme is taught in English?

Not necessarily. TUM has programmes taught in English, German, or both, so check the language requirements of your chosen programme.

Can Indian students work while studying at TUM?

Yes. International students can generally work in Germany subject to the conditions and limits of their residence status.

Does TUM have campuses outside Munich?

Yes. TUM has study and research locations, including Garching, Weihenstephan, Straubing, Heilbronn, Ottobrunn-Taufkirchen, and Singapore, depending on the programme.

Can I use the same requirements for every TUM Master's programme?

No. Master's programmes can have different academic, language, aptitude assessment, and additional test requirements.

Does TUM offer scholarships to international students?

Yes. TUM offers scholarships for eligible international students, while additional funding opportunities may also be available through external organisations.

Final Thoughts on Studying at the Technical University of Munich

TUM offers a wide range of Bachelor's and Master's programmes across technical, scientific, management, and interdisciplinary fields. For international applicants, the key is to evaluate the individual programme rather than relying on university-wide assumptions about eligibility, language, deadlines, or fees. Indian applicants should also plan country-specific documentation early, particularly where APS, VPD, or additional programme requirements apply.
