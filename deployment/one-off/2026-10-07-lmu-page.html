Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München (LMU Munich): Admissions, Fees & Requirements for Indian Students

LMU Munich is a public research university in Munich and a strong, research-intensive comprehensive university with broad subject coverage. Founded in 1472, it moved from Ingolstadt to Munich in 1826. LMU Munich is one of Germany's largest universities and is the largest university in Bavaria, with 52,658 students in winter semester 2025/26.

Around 22% of LMU's students are international, and the university is organised into 18 faculties. Its main address is Geschwister-Scholl-Platz 1, Munich.

Indian applicants must meet LMU's general and programme-specific admission requirements. Depending on the programme, applicants may need an APS certificate, language certification and additional selection or aptitude requirements. LMU does not have one universal application procedure for all programmes.

German for Bachelor's programmes

LMU requires German proficiency for Bachelor's programmes, with English-taught Master's programmes being the exception. Most LMU Bachelor's programmes are German-taught and require German-language proof. The exact level and accepted certificate depend on the programme and admission route.

LMU's general information describes B2 as the minimum evidence at application in certain unrestricted-admission cases, followed by the required higher-level certificate for registration.

🇬🇧

English-taught Master's programmes

English-taught Master's programmes generally do not require German for admission. LMU itself says the exception to its general German requirement is for Master's and doctoral programmes taught in English.

This is not a blanket rule for every programme: some programmes may have German-language electives or other programme-specific requirements. For example, LMU's Environment and Society programme states that German is not formally required, but German is useful for certain electives.

LMU does not have one university-wide IELTS or TOEFL score for every English-taught Master's; check the English requirement of your programme.

Eligibility

LMU Admission Requirements for Indian Students

Requirements differ between Bachelor's and Master's programmes, programme-specific requirements matter, and language requirements vary by programme. Some programmes have aptitude or selection procedures.

🎒

Bachelor's applicants

LMU's general international application page specifies the university entrance qualification, which may consist of a school-leaving certificate plus university entrance examination / higher education study evidence, depending on the applicant's qualification.

LMU also explicitly requires an APS certificate for applicants with Indian qualifications.

📚

Master's applicants

LMU's Master's guidance confirms that applicants with Indian university degrees should submit the APS certificate, along with university certificates and transcripts. Programme-specific requirements can add:

  • Motivation letter
  • CV
  • Aptitude test
  • Recommendation
  • Specific credits
  • Language proof
  • Selection interview
🧪

GRE, GMAT and other tests

LMU does not have a university-wide GATE or GMAT requirement. Some individual programmes may use GMAT or other tests as part of their admission procedure. For example, LMU's Business Education Master's programmes state that a GMAT result can be used in the admission process.

Some LMU Master's programmes may require or recommend the GRE; requirements are programme-specific. LMU's regular M.Sc. Economics, for example, states that GRE/GMAT are not required for application.

🎯

Numerus clausus (NC)

Popular programmes at LMU can be subject to admission restrictions, and LMU distinguishes national and local NC procedures.

APS verifies Indian educational documents. It should not be understood as the general mechanism by which LMU performs the final NC ranking.

Step by step

How to Apply to LMU from India

LMU explicitly says there is no single application procedure for all international students. International applicants generally apply to the International Office, some programmes have additional institute-level procedures, and a defined list of Master's programmes uses MoveIN.

  1. Choose your programme

    Start from the individual LMU programme page: language, intake, admission route and requirements are set per programme.

  2. Check programme-specific eligibility

    Compare your qualification with the programme's academic, language, and selection or aptitude requirements.

  3. Check whether APS is required

    LMU requires APS for applicants with Indian qualifications.

  4. Complete the programme's specified application procedure

    Prepare the documents your programme lists. Complete programme-specific preliminary documentation only if it is explicitly required.

  5. Submit to the responsible LMU office, faculty or admission platform

    Depending on the programme this may be the International Office, an institute-level procedure, or MoveIN for specified Master's programmes.

  6. Complete any aptitude or selection procedure

    Some programmes add tests, interviews or other selection steps.

  7. Receive admission

    Follow the instructions in your admission notice.

  8. Complete registration / enrolment

    Register within the deadline your admission notice gives.

Costs

LMU Tuition Fees and Semester Contribution

LMU currently does not generally charge tuition fees for its regular degree programmes, but students must pay the applicable semester contribution. Individual programme pages should be checked for programme-specific charges; LMU programme pages explicitly state no tuition fees while listing the semester contribution.

💶

Semester contribution: €97

LMU states that the basic contribution increased from €85 to €97 from summer semester 2026. For winter semester 2026/27, students pay €97.

🚆

No transport ticket included

For winter semester 2026/27, LMU states that the additional semester-ticket contribution has been suspended until further notice. The €97 semester contribution therefore does not include a public transport ticket, so budget for transport separately.

Living in Munich

Cost of Living and Housing in Munich

Munich is an expensive student city. Actual monthly costs depend heavily on rent, health insurance, food, transport and lifestyle. Students should prepare a realistic budget using current LMU and Munich Student Union guidance.

Scholarships at LMU

Deutschlandstipendium

Work & career

Working During and After Your Studies

💼

Work while studying

International students can work subject to residence rules. Current German government guidance says international students can generally work 140 full days or 280 half days per year, or alternatively up to 20 hours per week under the applicable rules.

🎓

After graduation

After successfully completing their degree in Germany, eligible international graduates can obtain a residence permit for up to 18 months to look for qualified employment.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About LMU Munich

Is LMU Munich a public university?

Yes. LMU Munich is a public research university in Munich, Germany.

Does LMU charge tuition fees?

LMU generally does not charge tuition fees for its regular degree programmes, but students pay a semester contribution of €97 (winter semester 2026/27). Check the individual programme page for programme-specific charges.

Does the semester contribution include a public transport ticket?

No. For winter semester 2026/27, LMU states that the additional semester-ticket contribution has been suspended until further notice.

Do I need German to study at LMU?

Most LMU Bachelor's programmes are German-taught and require German-language proof. English-taught Master's programmes generally do not require German for admission, but check your programme's own requirements.

Do Indian applicants need an APS certificate for LMU?

Yes. LMU requires an APS certificate for applicants with Indian qualifications.

Is there one LMU application portal for all programmes?

No. LMU has no single application procedure for all international students. Many apply to the International Office, some programmes have additional institute-level procedures, and specified Master's programmes use MoveIN.

Can I start at LMU in the summer semester?

The LMU International Office has both summer and winter application periods, but each programme decides whether it admits students in each semester.

Can Indian students work while studying at LMU?

Yes, subject to residence rules: generally 140 full days or 280 half days per year, or alternatively up to 20 hours per week under the applicable rules.

Can I stay in Germany after graduating from LMU?

Eligible international graduates can obtain a residence permit for up to 18 months after successfully completing their degree to look for qualified employment.
